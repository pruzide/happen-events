PROJECT INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Happen is a student demonstration project. Its sample events are fictional. Registrations do not issue tickets, reserve places at a real venue or collect payment. Do not submit sensitive or real-world personal information for these fictional events.

Information collected

If you submit the registration form, the backend stores your name, email address, phone number, event identifier, generated reference and timestamps. The form is protected by a bot verification service when deployed publicly. The browser stores only event identifiers and dates to display your sign-ups under My events; no name, email, phone number or confirmation reference is stored in local storage.

Purpose and retention

Registration details are used solely to demonstrate sign-ups and prevent duplicates. Records are automatically removed 30 days after each event ends by a scheduled cleanup job (subject to the deployment's scheduled task operating). Short-lived pseudonymous submission rate-limit records are also stored and deleted. This project does not send marketing messages or share registration details with other visitors.

Your choices

The My events view shows sign-ups made using this browser. Clearing this site’s browser data removes that local list, but does not cancel a registration or delete its database record. For deletion requests, contact the site operator using the contact address displayed below. Do not include personal information in public GitHub issues.

Contact

The site owner must configure a privacy contact before enabling public registrations.
